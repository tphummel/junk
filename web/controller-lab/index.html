    Joy-Con (Switch 1)
    
          
      
            
    • ConfirmedTwo paired Joy-Cons merge into one controller, and you can split them again: hold Capture (left) and Home (right) for about 3 seconds. Do it again to merge.
      • 
            
    • ReportedPair each one separately: hold the small sync button on its rail until the lights sweep, then pick "Joy-Con (L)" or "(R)" under Settings, Bluetooth. Needs iPadOS 16 or later.
      • 
            
    • LabWhen Joy-Cons merge or split, controllers disappear and new ones appear. Press a button to register the new one, and use Forget on the grey cards that are left.
      • 
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    8BitDo SN30 (original, not Pro)
    
          
      
            
    • ConfirmedPair it with the iPad using Start + X, then choose it under Settings, Bluetooth.
      • 
            
    • ReportedThis is the Bluetooth SN30. The SNES/SFC 2.4G edition has no Bluetooth (it uses a USB receiver) and will not pair with an iPad.
      • 
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    8BitDo SN30 Pro
    
          
      
            
    • ReportedHold B + Start to turn it on, then hold the pair button for 3 seconds until the lights rotate left to right, then pair "8BitDo SN30 Pro" in Bluetooth. 8BitDo's manual says this is needed the first time only.
      • 
            
    • Reported8BitDo's Apple page mentions D-Input mode for some models. If a pad shows numbered buttons, try the other mode and compare the mapping line in Stats.
      • 
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Switch 2 Joy-Con and Pro Controller
    
          
      
            
    • UnknownI found no confirmation that these pair with an iPad, and one forum post says a Switch 2 Pro Controller did not work on an iPad Pro.
      • 
            
    • ReportedTo try: take it off the console and switch the console off, hold its SYNC button until the lights flash, then look under Settings, Bluetooth. If it pairs but the lab never sees it, check Settings, General, Game Controllers: if it is not listed there, Safari cannot see it either.
      • 
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Pairing in general
    
          
      
            
    • ReportedA controller paired to another device may reconnect there when switched on. Turn Bluetooth off on that device, and hold the controller's pairing button to override the old pairing.
      • 
            
    • ReportedIf a pairing misbehaves, tap the (i) next to it in Settings, Bluetooth, choose Forget This Device, and pair again.
      • 
            
    • LabA controller appears here only after you press one of its buttons on this page, and the first to press is P1.
      • 
            
    • LabPair two identical controllers one at a time and label them (the iPad lists both under the same name). If both drop and return, the lab may swap their slots: Forget both and register them again in order.
      •
