x4 reading report

Turn the reading data on an Xteink X4 into a report. Copy the .metadata and .system folders from the SD card, then drop both here. Everything is decoded in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Drop the .metadata and .system folders here Or a saved project .json to reopen a report.

What gets read

Only the small files that hold reading data: books.bin, recent.bin, and each book's statistics.bin, progress.bin, book.bin and cover.jpg, plus the device totals and state. Chapter caches, images, settings and WiFi credentials are skipped and never read.
