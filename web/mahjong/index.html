Mahjong

Card

An original card in the style of the NMJL card, not the real one. Colors a, b and c are three different suits.

How this plays

  • Four players, 152 tiles: three suits, winds, dragons, 8 flowers and 8 jokers. You hold 13 tiles (14 if you deal).
  • Charleston: pass three tiles right, across and left (no jokers). After that you may vote for a second one; every player must agree.
  • Turns: draw, then discard. Tap a tile and press Discard.
  • Calling: take another player's discard to expose a pung (3), kong (4) or quint (5) that a hand on the card needs. Jokers can stand in for any tile in a set of 3 or more, never in a single or pair, and you cannot call a joker.
  • Swap: on your turn, tap an exposed joker to swap in the real tile from your rack.
  • Mahjong: 14 tiles that match a hand on the card. Hands marked concealed cannot have exposed sets.
  • Payment: the winner is paid the hand's value. Off a discard, the discarder pays double and the others pay once. Self-drawn, everyone pays double.
  • Not included yet: blind passes, courtesy pass, dead hands, jokerless bonus, the real NMJL card.
