Celebration

Style sheet

A red field, white and gold type, and hairline grids around spaced-out caps, inspired by the Sierra Nevada Celebration can. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="cel" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed cel- and every custom property --cel-. Plain element selectors only apply under .cel.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/celebration/celebration.css">
<body class="cel">
  <div class="cel-panel">
    <h1 class="cel-title cel-title--lg">Hello</h1>
  </div>
</body>

Components read context variables (--cel-fg, --cel-line, --cel-accent, --cel-heading, --cel-field), so the same markup works on the page and inside a .cel-panel, where the panel flips them to white and gold on red. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page.

Palette

Values below are read from the live sheet, so they follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines and controls 3.

Type

Display type is a heavy slab serif in caps (Clarendon, Rockwell, Roboto Slab or Georgia, whichever the device has). Labels and values are small caps with wide tracking, which is what gives the grids their feel. The script flourish is italic.

Grid

Ruled cells with spaced caps, the can's 12 fluid ounces | ale | alc. 6.8% by vol. strip. Cells share their lines however they wrap. Use cel-cell--wide to span a full row, cel-grid--2, --3 or --4 for fixed columns (two on a phone), or no modifier to fit as many as the width allows.

Controls

Buttons are gold with a hard red-black edge; cel-button--red and cel-button--ghost step down in weight. Inputs are 44px tall with the same hairline.
