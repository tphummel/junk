Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="bb" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed bb- and every custom property --bb-. Plain element selectors only apply under .bb.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/blockbuster/blockbuster.css">
<body class="bb">
  <div class="bb-panel">
    <h1 class="bb-title bb-title--lg">Hello</h1>
  </div>
</body>

Components read context variables (--bb-fg, --bb-line, --bb-accent, --bb-heading, --bb-field), so the same markup works on the page and inside a .bb-panel, .bb-wall, .bb-carpet or .bb-coupon, which flip them for their own color. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page. The ticket, wall, carpet, VHS case and coupon keep their colors in both themes; the page background, text and lines change.

Palette

Values below are read from the live sheet, so they follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines and controls 3. Pink, teal and purple appear only in the carpet and flourishes, never as text.

Type

Display type is a condensed heavy sans in caps (Haettenschweiler, Franklin Gothic Condensed, Roboto Condensed or Arial Narrow, whichever the device has), like the logo. bb-new is the slanted black italic of the new-release sign. Labels and values are small caps with tracking.

Ticket, sign

The ticket stub from the logo: blue, with a punched notch on the left, nicks on the right, a gold keyline and gold caps. It sizes to its text and tilts a little; bb-ticket--flat straightens it, --sm shrinks it, --sub sets small spaced body caps. bb-sign is the genre header on the wall, and bb-sign--new is the yellow one.

Grid

Ruled cells with spaced caps, for stats, receipts and rental details. Cells share their lines however they wrap. Use bb-cell--wide to span a full row, bb-grid--2, --3 or --4 for fixed columns (two on a phone), or no modifier to fit as many as the width allows.

Controls

Buttons are yellow with a blue edge in condensed caps; bb-button--blue, --red and --ghost step through the other looks, and --block fills the row. Inputs are 44px tall.

Badge, dividers

Badges are small caps in a pill. bb-film is a blue strip with sprocket holes (gold on a panel or the carpet); bb-divider is two plain lines.
