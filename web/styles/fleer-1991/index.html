Fleer'91

Bright yellow cards, triple black rules, a small first name over a big heavy serif last name, and a blue and red card back with light blue stats. Inspired by the 1991 Fleer baseball set. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="fl" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed fl- and every custom property --fl-. Plain element selectors only apply under .fl.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/fleer-1991/fleer-1991.css">
<body class="fl">
  <div class="fl-banner fl-banner--center">
    <h1 class="fl-title fl-title--lg">Hello</h1>
  </div>
</body>

Components read context variables (--fl-fg, --fl-line, --fl-accent, --fl-heading, --fl-field), so the same markup works on the page and inside a .fl-card, .fl-banner or .fl-panel, which flip them for their own color. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page. Cards, banners, stat tables and the panel keep their colors in both themes; the page background, text and lines change.

Palette

Values below are read from the live sheet, so they follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines and controls 3.

Type, logo

Display type is a heavy high-contrast serif in caps (Bodoni, Didot, Playfair, or Georgia where none is installed). fl-kicker is the small first name; fl-label is small spaced caps. The logo is italic black on white with a red year; fl-logo--lg enlarges it.

Tables

Light blue field, blue header rule, pink highlight columns (fl-hl) and ink type, the same in both themes. fl-table is page size; fl-stats is the small one from the card back. Wrap a table in fl-table-wrap if it can overflow a phone, and right-align numbers with fl-num.

Grid

Ruled boxes for stat lines. Cells share their lines however they wrap. fl-cell--wide spans a row, fl-cell--tall gives height, fl-cell--left aligns content left; fl-grid--2, --3 and --4 set columns (two on a phone), or no modifier fits as many as the width allows. fl-value--xl is the big number, --plain sets it in body type.

Controls

Buttons are yellow with a black edge and heavy serif caps; fl-button--blue, --red and --ghost step through the other looks, and --block fills the row. Inputs are 44px tall with a 2px edge.

Badge, divider

Badges are small caps in a pill. fl-divider is the card's triple rule: one thick line over two thin ones. A plain <hr> under .fl looks the same.
