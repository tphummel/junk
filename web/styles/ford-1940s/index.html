An Industrial CityFord 1940s style sheet

Rouge

Black ink on white paper, inspired by a 1940s Ford Motor Company plant-tour map: a reversed stencil title bar, heavy roads and thin property lines, hatched building areas, a boxed legend and numbered gates. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load. Dark mode prints the same page as a negative.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="fd" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed fd- and every custom property --fd-. Plain element selectors only apply under .fd.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/ford-1940s/ford-1940s.css">
<body class="fd">
  <div class="fd-plate">
    <span class="fd-bar">Hello</span>
    <div class="fd-plate__body">...</div>
  </div>
</body>

Components read context variables (--fd-fg, --fd-line, --fd-accent, --fd-heading, --fd-field), so the same markup works on the page and inside a reversed .fd-bar or .fd-reverse, where ink and paper trade places. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page.

Palette

Two colors, ink and paper, plus a dark gray for fine print. Light is black on white; dark swaps them. Values below are read from the live sheet, so they follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines and controls 3.

Type

Display type is a heavy stencil or slab in caps (Stencil, Rockwell Extra Bold, Roboto Slab or Courier New, whichever the device has). Labels are small condensed caps with wide tracking, like the place names on the map. Body copy is a book serif, and captions are italic.

Bar, reverse

The reversed title strip: paper-colored stencil caps on solid ink, with fd-bar__sub for a smaller second line. fd-bar--sm is the compact size, --left aligns left and --inline sizes it to its text. fd-reverse applies the same swap to any block.

Plate, card

The plate is the printed map's frame: a heavy rule with a thin keyline set just outside it. Put a bar first for the title strip and fd-plate__foot last for the banner line. fd-card is the quiet version for content on the page, with one thin rule and a road along the top.

Lines

Four kinds of line, all in the line color. fd-road is the heavy street (fd-road--thin for a side street), fd-rule a pair of property lines, fd-rail a railroad with ties, and fd-route a dashed path that ends in an arrowhead (use a <div>, since an <hr> cannot draw the head).

Fills, tags

Building areas are hatched, never gray. fd-hatch is the default, with --dense, --cross, --dots and --solid variants. Lettering on a hatch goes in an fd-tag, which sits on plain paper so it stays readable.

Gate, legend

Gates are numbered squares; fd-gate--round and --solid change the shape and fill. The legend is a boxed key: wrap each row in fd-key with a modifier (--route, --building, --road or --rail) and an empty <i> for the swatch.

Grid

Ruled cells with spaced caps, like a fact sheet. Cells share their lines however they wrap. Use fd-cell--wide to span a full row, fd-grid--2, --3 or --4 for fixed columns (two on a phone), or no modifier to fit as many as the width allows.

Table

A ledger: heavy rules above and below, a solid header row and hairlines between rows. Wrap it in fd-table-wrap if it can overflow a phone. Numbers right-align with fd-num.

Controls

Buttons are solid ink with a hard offset edge that closes when pressed; fd-button--outline and fd-button--ghost (dashed) step down in weight. Inputs are 44px tall with a 2px rule.

Badge

Badges are small caps in a square box; fd-badge--solid fills it.
