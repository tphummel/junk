Style sheet

Topps 1991

Inspired by a 1991-design baseball card: white stock around a colored team frame, a script team banner and gold anniversary seal on the front, orange-red cardstock with a cream stat plate on the back. Plain CSS with system fonts, no images or scripts.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="tp" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed tp- and every custom property --tp-. Plain element selectors only apply under .tp.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/topps-1991/topps-1991.css">
<body class="tp">
  <div class="tp-card tp-card--sky">
    <div class="tp-card__face">
      <div class="tp-card__photo"><img src="..." alt="..."></div>
      <div class="tp-card__foot">...</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</body>

Components read context variables (--tp-fg, --tp-edge, --tp-accent, --tp-heading, --tp-field), so the same markup works on the page and inside a .tp-reverse block. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that. Only the page changes with the theme: cards are printed objects and keep their colors.

Palette

Values are read from the live sheet. Page colors follow the theme; card colors do not. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines and controls 3.

Card front

A tp-card is a 5:7 white card. Everything inside is sized from the card's own width, so set --tp-card-w (default 16rem) and it all scales. Inside: tp-card__face is the colored frame, tp-card__photo holds an <img> (or a placeholder), tp-card__team is the script banner, and tp-card__foot is the name strip with tp-card__pos and tp-card__name. tp-logo and tp-seal placed directly in the card sit at its top left.

Team frames

The team modifier sets six tokens: --tp-frame (the band), --tp-trim, --tp-line, --tp-banner, --tp-script and --tp-halo. Seven ship: --sky, --steel, --crimson, --forest, --purple, --gold and --navy. Override the tokens on any card for another team.

Seal, logo

The seal is a gold disc: a number in <b> and the ribbon text in <i>. Size it with --tp-seal-size or tp-seal--sm and --lg. The logo is heavy italic type outlined in white; it takes whatever text you give it.

Card back

A landscape tp-back on orange-red cardstock, with a cream tp-back__plate inside it. data-mark on the plate sets the faint oversized mark behind the stats. The head strip holds a tp-number, the name and team; then tp-back__bio, a tp-back__title, a tp-stats table, and the tp-back__blurb. tp-lead marks a league leader in italics, and tp-tie adds the diamond. The back keeps a minimum width, so wrap it in tp-back-wrap and it scrolls on a phone.

Panel, reverse

For page content that is not a card: tp-panel is the cream plate with a navy edge and corner dots, with an optional tp-panel__head peach strip. tp-reverse swaps to navy stock with cream type, and links turn gold.

Ribbon, bar

A tp-ribbon is a swallowtail title; --gold and --red are fixed fills. tp-bar is the stripe band from the foot of a card, as a divider (tp-bar--thin is narrower); it takes its color from --tp-frame, so set that on it or a wrapper to match a team.

Binder

A page of sleeves: tp-binder holds tp-slot pockets (fit as many as the width allows, or add tp-binder--3 for three columns). Cards fill their slot. tp-slot--empty is a gap in the set, and tp-slot__no numbers it.

Controls

Buttons are italic heavy caps with a hard shadow that closes when pressed; --outline, --gold and --ghost (dashed) vary it. Inputs are 44px tall with a heavy bottom edge.

Badge

Pill-shaped small caps; --solid fills with ink and --gold with gold.
