Los Angeles

Blue city signs, green freeway guides, double yellow lines and white letters on a dry hillside. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="la" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed la- and every custom property --la-. Plain element selectors only apply under .la.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/los-angeles/los-angeles.css">
<body class="la">
  <div class="la-sign">
    <span class="la-seal" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    Hyde Park
  </div>
</body>

Components read context variables (--la-fg, --la-line, --la-accent, --la-heading, --la-field), so the same markup works on the page and inside a .la-panel, .la-sign, .la-highway or .la-hollywood, which flip them for their own color. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page. The signs, the road strip and the hillside keep their colors in both themes; the page background, text and lines change.

Palette

Values below are read from the live sheet, so they follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines and controls 3. Gold, asphalt and the sky appear only in signs, the road strip and the hillside scene, never as text on the page.

Type

Sign lettering is a clean, wide-set sans (Clearview, Highway Gothic or Frutiger where installed, otherwise Segoe UI, Helvetica or Arial), like the signs. Labels and values are small caps with tracking. The hillside letters use a heavy block face (Impact or Arial Black).

The city is a grid of long boulevards, each one with a blue sign at the corner telling you which neighborhood you just drove into.

City sign, seal

The blue neighborhood sign: white edge, rounded corners, white lettering. Put la-seal first inside it and it rides the top edge; the sign makes room. la-sign--left aligns left, --inline sizes to its text, and a <small> inside sets a quiet second line. The seal is a plain stand-in and also works alone, with la-seal--sm or --lg.

Street sign

The street-name blade. The name goes in la-street__name, the block number under the left end in __block and the suffix at the right in __suffix.

Freeway sign

The green overhead guide sign: a white keyline inside rounded corners, wide white lettering. la-highway__dir is the heading (SOUTH, EXIT 3), la-highway__row holds a la-shield and the __to destination, and __arrows holds la-arrow elements (--up, --left, --right turn them, --sm shrinks them). la-highway__exit is the yellow tab.

Hillside

Sky, a dry hillside and big white block letters standing on it, for a page header or a banner. The letters go in la-hollywood__sign; labels and copy inside are light on the hill. It is the same in both themes.

Panel, card

A blue field with a white edge and a faint sheen. la-panel--flat drops the sheen and la-panel--green switches to freeway green. la-card is the quiet version for content on the page background (la-card--green for a green top rule).

Grid

Ruled cells with spaced caps, for stats and details. Cells share their lines however they wrap. Use la-cell--wide to span a full row, la-grid--2, --3 or --4 for fixed columns (two on a phone), or no modifier to fit as many as the width allows.

Controls

Buttons are blue with a white edge; la-button--green, --gold, --red and --ghost step through the other looks, and --block fills the row. Inputs are 44px tall.

Badge, dividers

Badges are small caps in a pill. la-road is an asphalt strip with a double yellow line and a dashed white edge (decorative); la-divider is a plain double rule, and a bare <hr> under .la draws the double yellow.
