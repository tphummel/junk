Style sheet

Fleer Tradition

Flat team colors in a cream border, wide black-caps names, kraft-cardboard backs with a home-plate number and a cartoon, inspired by the 2000 Fleer Tradition baseball cards. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="ft" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed ft- and every custom property --ft-.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/fleer-tradition-2000/fleer-tradition-2000.css">
<body class="ft">
  <article class="ft-card ft-c-blue">
    <header class="ft-card-head">
      <h3 class="ft-card-name">Tom Davey</h3>
      <p class="ft-card-sub">Seattle Mariners &bull; Pitcher</p>
    </header>
  </article>
</body>

The page is a binder page of card stock and changes with light and dark. Cards, backs and sleeves are printed cardboard: they keep cream, kraft and ink in both modes, like cards on a dark table. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>. Photos, logos and cartoons are slots: put an <img> in them, or leave them empty for a placeholder.

Palette

Values are read from the live sheet and follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines 3.

Type

Names are wide heavy caps (Eurostile or Arial Black, whichever the device has). Team and position lines are small spaced caps. Backs use a narrow sans, and the signature is a script face.

Card front

A team-color field in a cream border (thicker at the foot). Six colors: ft-c-yellow, blue, red, green, purple, orange; each sets the text color that reads on it. Parts: ft-card-head (name and team line), ft-logo, ft-photo, ft-action (shown in black and white), ft-sig and ft-brand.

Card back

Maroon border, kraft stock. ft-num is the home-plate card number, ft-bio the height, weight and birth lines, ft-blurb a paragraph beside a ft-toon cartoon box, then a stat table and ft-fine print with a span brand tag. Add data-team="Pirates" for the faint watermark.

Stat table

On a card back the table inherits the kraft ink and sits under a black caption bar. ft-table--page puts the same table on the page in a cream box ruled in ink, with room to breathe. Put numbers in plain cells; they align right, and the first two columns align left.

Binder page

Nine-pocket pages: ft-binder is the page, ft-sleeve a clear pocket that holds a card or a back at full width.

Controls

Buttons are blue like the Fleer badge, with a hard ink shadow that presses in. --paper is cream, --ghost is an outline. Inputs sit on cream with an ink border.

Badge, divider

Badges are small caps in a pill. The divider is a thin rule over a thick one.
