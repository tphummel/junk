Style sheet

Topps 1989

White card stock, team-color frames, script team names and name ribbons on the front; pink panels, stat lines and a monthly scoreboard on the back. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="tp" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed tp- and every custom property --tp-. Plain element selectors only apply under .tp.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/topps-1989/topps-1989.css">
<body class="tp">
  <div class="tp-card tp-t-reds">
    <div class="tp-card-frame">
      <img class="tp-card-photo" src="player.jpg" alt="">
      <span class="tp-card-team">Reds</span>
    </div>
    <span class="tp-card-name">Frank Williams</span>
  </div>
</body>

The page is the desk the cards sit on, and it changes with light and dark. Cards are printed cardboard: they keep their white stock, pink and ink in both modes. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page.

Palette

Values are read from the live sheet and follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines 3.

Type

Headings and ribbons are heavy sans caps (Arial Black or Helvetica). Team names use a bold italic script, and the back of the card sets the player's name in a serif with small caps. Fonts are system stacks, so the look varies a little by device.

Card front

A 5:7 card sized by its width, so everything inside scales with it. Inside tp-card-frame go the photo (tp-card-photo, an <img> or empty for the stand-in field), the tp-card-logo and the tp-card-team script. The tp-card-name ribbon overlaps the frame. tp-card-flag is the "#1 Draft Pick" style corner flag. Team classes (next section) set the colors.

Each sets the frame, the script fill and edge, and the ribbon with a text color that reads on it. The colors are approximations by eye. Put one on a card, badge, chip or table row.

Binder page

Card back

Landscape, black border, pink panels. The head holds the numbered circle (tp-back-num), the name and position (tp-back-name) and the ball logo (tp-back-logo), then tp-back-bio and a tp-back-title over the tp-back-stats table (tp-stats, a tp-total row for career totals and <em> for league leaders). tp-back-trivia is the deeper pink strip, tp-scoreboard the monthly box and tp-back-fine the copyright line. The stats table scrolls sideways on a phone.

Controls

Buttons are Topps-red pills with a hard ink edge; tp-button--paper and tp-button--ghost step down in weight. Inputs are 44px tall on card stock.

Badge, divider

Badges are small caps in a pill, ringed in the team's frame color. The divider is a double rule in the same color.
