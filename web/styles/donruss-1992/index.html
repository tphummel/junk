White card stock, sky-blue gradient bars, a chiseled name plate and a red-and-blue stat back, inspired by 1992 Donruss baseball cards. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="don" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed don- and every custom property --don-. Plain element selectors only apply under .don.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/donruss-1992/donruss-1992.css">
<body class="don">
  <div class="don-card">
    <div class="don-card-head">Donruss 1992</div>
    <div class="don-photo"><img src="player.jpg" alt=""></div>
    <div class="don-plate"><b class="don-name">Jody Reed</b><span class="don-sub">Red Sox • Second Base</span></div>
  </div>
</body>

The page is the binder, and it changes with light and dark. Cards are printed stock: they keep white, ink, sky blue and red in both modes, like cards on a dark table. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page.

Palette

Values are read from the live sheet and follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines 3 (the dark-mode line is a 4.3 divider, above that).

Type

Names and headings are condensed bold caps (Arial Narrow, Roboto Condensed or Impact, whichever the device has). Small print is the plain system sans. Labels are tiny, widely tracked caps like the "D O N R U S S" strip.

Bar

The sky-blue gradient stripe, dark at the top and pale at the bottom, with spaced caps. Use it as a header or divider. The same gradient is the --don-bar token.

Controls

Buttons are the sky-blue gradient with a hard ink edge; don-button--red, --paper and --ghost vary it. Inputs are 44px tall on white stock.

Badge, divider

Badges are small caps in a pill, blue by default or don-badge--red. The divider is a thin gradient bar between two hairlines.
