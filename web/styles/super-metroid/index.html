Style sheet

Super Metroid

Black box art, a chiseled red-to-yellow title, red label tags and orange bullets, with pink HUD slots, energy tanks and riveted metal. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="sm" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed sm- and every custom property --sm-. Plain element selectors only apply under .sm.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/super-metroid/super-metroid.css">
<body class="sm">
  <div class="sm-panel">
    <h1 class="sm-title sm-title--lg">Zebes</h1>
  </div>
</body>

Light mode is the grey cart label and dark mode is the box art. Metal panels (sm-panel) stay dark in both, like the HUD does, and flip the context variables (--sm-fg, --sm-line, --sm-accent, --sm-heading, --sm-hud, --sm-field) so slots, tables and inputs inside them just work. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page.

Palette

Values are read from the live sheet, so they follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines and controls 3.

Type

The title is heavy condensed italic caps (Impact or Haettenschweiler) filled red to yellow with a stacked dark-red extrusion; use it for a short logo-like phrase. Headings under .sm use the same face in a flat color. HUD labels and numbers are bold mono with wide tracking.

Panel

Riveted green-grey metal with a bevel. sm-panel--black is the box art's flat black. sm-card is the quiet version for content on the page background.

Metal panel

Light text, orange accents, pink HUD borders.

Black panel

Flat black, for hero areas.

Card

Surface color and a rule, for content that should stay quiet.

HUD

Separate boxes with pink borders, like the item slots across the top of the screen. sm-slot--active is the selected one in green, sm-slot--wide spans a row, sm-hud--2, --3 or --4 fix the columns (two on a phone), and no modifier fits as many as the width allows.

Tanks, meter

Energy tanks are a row of small squares; add sm-tank--full for each one held. The meter is segmented and takes its fill from --sm-pct.

Table

Red header like a label tag, mono values in the accent color. Wrap it in sm-table-wrap if it can overflow a phone. Numbers right-align with sm-num.

Controls

Buttons are red label tags with a hard dark-red edge; sm-button--orange and sm-button--hud step down in weight. Inputs are 44px tall with pink borders and mono type.

Badge, stripes

Badges are small mono caps. The stripes are the grey bars that thin out down the box art's right edge, used as a divider.
