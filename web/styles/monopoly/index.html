Style sheet

Monopoly

A mint board, black ink, color-group bars and cream title deeds, inspired by a vintage Parker Brothers set. Plain CSS, no fonts or scripts to load, light and dark.

Use it

Link the sheet from any page on this site, then add class="mon" to <body> for the page defaults. Nothing changes until you do: every class is prefixed mon- and every custom property --mon-. Plain element selectors only apply under .mon.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/monopoly/monopoly.css">
<body class="mon">
  <div class="mon-deed mon-g-blue">
    <div class="mon-deed-head"><small>Title deed</small><b>Park Place</b></div>
  </div>
</body>

The page is the board, and it changes with light and dark. Deeds, spaces, cards and bills are printed paper: they keep cream and ink in both modes, like pieces on a dark table. Force a theme with data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>; the switch above does that for this page.

Palette

Values are read from the live sheet and follow the theme switch. Ratios are WCAG contrast against the stated background; text needs 4.5, lines 3.

Type

Headings and labels are geometric sans caps with wide tracking (Futura, Century Gothic or Trebuchet, whichever the device has). Deed text is a plain serif.

Color groups

Eight groups, from the vintage set: mon-g-purple, lblue, magenta, orange, red, yellow, green, blue. Add one to a mon-bar, mon-deed, mon-space or table chip and it sets the bar color and a text color that reads on it.

Chance, Community Chest

Salmon Chance and gold Community Chest, landscape like the real ones: an italic script title, bold caps text and a line of fine print. Put a mon-card-mark (a glyph or drawing) beside the text if you want an illustration.

Dice

Set data-n from 1 to 6 on a mon-die with nine empty <i> children; the sheet shows the right pips.

Table

A ruled property list on paper. Wrap it in mon-table-wrap if it can overflow a phone. Numbers right-align with mon-num; mon-chip inside a mon-g-* row cell shows the group color.

Controls

Buttons are Go-arrow red with a hard ink edge; mon-button--paper and mon-button--ghost step down in weight. Inputs are 44px tall on paper.

Badge, divider

Badges are small caps in a pill. The divider is two hairlines, like the board's ruled edges.
