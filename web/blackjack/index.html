Rules of thumb

  • Hard 17 or more always stands. Soft 19 and 20 stand unless the chart says otherwise.
  • With three or more cards you cannot double, split or surrender: take the hit / stand half of the code (D = hit, Ds = stand, R = the letter after it).
  • Never take insurance or "even money". It is a side bet that costs you on average.
  • Chart colors never carry meaning: letters, hatching, boxes and black fill do, so it prints in black and white.
