How the numbers work
  • Invested: purchase price, every house, and the 10% interest when you lift a mortgage.
  • Returned: rent collected plus improvements sold back (at half cost).
  • Break even when returned reaches invested. ROI is (returned − invested) ÷ invested.
  • A mortgage is a loan: the cash is not counted as income, only the interest is a cost. Outstanding loans are shown separately.
  • Rents to break even uses your average rent collected so far, or the current rent you entered.
  • Improvement levels: 1–4 houses, 5 hotel, and in Mega 6 skyscraper. Edit the cost per house on any property.
