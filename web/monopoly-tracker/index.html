How the numbers work
  • Invested: purchase price, every house, and the 10% interest when you lift a mortgage.
  • Returned: rent collected plus improvements sold back (at half cost).
  • Break even when returned reaches invested. ROI is (returned − invested) ÷ invested.
  • A mortgage is a loan: the cash is not counted as income, only the interest is a cost. Outstanding loans are shown separately.
  • Rent is taken from the card and your game: improvement level, set bonuses (standard: double for a full unimproved set; Mega: 2+ owned and full set have their own rents), railroads and utilities by number owned. You only enter the dice total for a utility.
  • Rents to break even uses your average rent collected so far, or what the card pays now.
  • Improvement levels: 1–4 houses, 5 hotel, and in Mega 6 skyscraper (Mega: 1–4 houses, hotel, skyscraper; a railroad takes one train depot, which doubles its rent).
