How the numbers work
  • Invested: purchase price, every house, and the 10% interest when you lift a mortgage.
  • Returned: rent collected plus improvements sold back (at half cost).
  • Game left to break even is your net loss across the whole game (invested less returned, never below zero). Owed by unpaid properties adds up what each property you own that hasn't paid back still owes, so a property that has paid off doesn't offset the others. They differ by the profit on paid-off properties, net of any loss on traded-away ones.
  • Break even when returned reaches invested. ROI is (returned − invested) ÷ invested.
  • A mortgage is a loan: the cash is not counted as income, only the interest is a cost. Outstanding loans are shown separately.
  • Repairs: the Repairs button charges a Chance or Community Chest repairs card for every house, hotel, skyscraper and train depot you own. A hotel replaces its houses and a skyscraper replaces its hotel, as on the board. Each property's share goes in its history and adds to its cost basis, so it counts toward what you need to break even.
  • Trades: the cost basis of what you give (price paid, less improvements sold back) plus cash you pay, less cash you receive, becomes the cost basis of what you receive, split evenly. Properties traded away stay in your totals, and basis moved between them is not counted twice.
  • Rent is taken from the card and your game: improvement level, set bonuses (standard: double for a full unimproved set; Mega: one shy of the full set pays double the site rent, and the full set pays triple), railroads and utilities by number owned. You only enter the dice total for a utility.
  • Rents to break even uses your average rent collected so far, or what the card pays now.
  • Improvement levels: 1–4 houses, 5 hotel, and in Mega 6 skyscraper (Mega: 1–4 houses, hotel, skyscraper; a railroad takes one train depot, which doubles its rent).
